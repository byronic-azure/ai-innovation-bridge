THE OCTAVE

eight faculties · corpus centroid · per-document overlay

Each faculty is a stem lexicon plus structural code signals — asserts, proof checks, hash calls feed confidence; health/status probes feed awareness; generate / pipeline / Llama calls feed intelligence. Density is hits per thousand tokens, normalised to the corpus peak, so 100 means "the most of this faculty anywhere in the corpus".
